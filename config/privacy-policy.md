## 2. Personal Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- **Account information:** username, email address, encrypted or hashed
  password, account preferences, and authentication information.
- **API and service information:** API keys issued by us, selected models,
  token usage, request status, and related billing records.
- **Prompt and output data:** text or other content submitted through the API
  and responses returned by model providers. This content must be transmitted
  to the selected model provider to perform the Service.
- **Payment information:** purchase amount, currency, transaction identifier,
  payment status, refunds, and dispute information. We do not store complete
  payment card numbers.
- **Communications:** support requests, emails, feedback, and other messages
  you send to us.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- IP address, browser type, operating system, device and network information,
  language, and approximate time zone.
- Login activity, pages visited, model usage, token consumption, request
  timestamps, error records, security events, and performance information.
- Cookies and similar technologies required for sessions, authentication,
  security, language, and interface preferences.

We do not request precise device location and do not currently use advertising
cookies.

## 5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral
advertising. We disclose information only as necessary:

- **Model providers:** prompts, required request parameters, and generated
  responses are processed by the provider selected for a request. Current
  providers may include DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Zhipu AI, Alibaba Cloud Model
  Studio, and MiniMax. Their processing is also governed by their own privacy
  terms.
- **Infrastructure and security providers:** Oracle Cloud hosts application
  infrastructure, and Cloudflare provides DNS, content delivery, security, and
  email routing services.
- **Payment providers:** Stripe, Creem, or Waffo Pancake may process payment,
  billing, fraud, tax, refund, and dispute information, depending on the
  payment method offered at checkout. Payment card data is submitted directly
  to the relevant payment provider and is not stored on our servers.
- **Professional and legal recipients:** advisers, regulators, courts, law
  enforcement, or other parties when disclosure is required by applicable law
  or is reasonably necessary to protect users, the Service, or legal rights.
- **Business transfers:** a successor in connection with a merger,
  acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of the Service, subject to
  continued protection and legally required notice.
- **With your direction or consent:** when you ask us to disclose information
  or otherwise provide valid consent.

We require service providers to handle information only for authorized
purposes and to apply appropriate safeguards.

## 6. Data Security

We use HTTPS/TLS in transit, password hashing, access controls, credential
separation, network protections, backups, monitoring, and security logging.
Upstream provider credentials are not disclosed to users.

No system is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your
password and API keys and must notify us promptly if you suspect unauthorized
access. If a security incident is legally reportable, we will notify affected
users and authorities within the period required by applicable law.

| Data type | Typical retention |
| --- | --- |
| Account information | While active and up to 30 days after deletion |

When retention ends, information is deleted or de-identified.
